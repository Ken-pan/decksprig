Your documents stay with you

Version 0.1.0 · 14 October 2026

The local edition stores documents, history and imported originals on your computer. The browser edition stores documents and history in this browser. There is no automatic document upload or project analytics SDK.

Optional connections

AI requests send your prompt, selected document context and relevant tool results to your chosen provider. Online photos, fonts, cloud imports and remote prototypes contact their respective services. These services have their own privacy terms and retention practices.

Keep a backup

Export a Deck JSON backup before clearing site data or changing browsers. Before sharing a presentation, check notes, hidden slides, source URLs, reference images and embedded media for private information.

Hosted copies

Ken Pan operates the official demo at decksprig-kens-toolbox.netlify.app, hosted by Netlify. There is no document backend, project analytics or enabled photo-search proxy. Netlify/CDN logs can still contain IP addresses and request paths. We cannot independently verify or promise provider retention. See Netlify’s privacy policy. Other hosted copies have their own operator and practices.

Maintained by Ken Pan. For source or official-demo privacy questions, request a private contact route in the issue tracker without personal data, private files or credentials.
